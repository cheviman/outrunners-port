OutRunners 1.1.2: a racing wheel's rotation and an INPUT TEST in STEERING, and the game's whole picture on a
4:3 Android. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `orunners.zip` (MAME's ROM
set, World) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts. The devices' games
(Switch, Android, Anbernic) are made again with the tools in the package.

**Fixed**

- **Android on a 4:3 screen** (#15): a handheld narrower than 3:2 (1280x960, for one) shows the game's
  4:3 picture filling the screen; it showed WIDE 16:9, smaller and with bands above and below. A tablet keeps
  16:9, the picture at the top and the controls under it.
- **Building from the source on the newest Ubuntu** (#8, #9): GCC 15 and Python 3.14 build it and run its
  tools; the boot's writes where the board maps nothing are counted, not printed as errors, and
  `check_scenarios.py` names the executable it misses.

**New in OPTIONS > STEERING**

- **WHEEL ROTATION** (#16): a racing wheel's own turn from end to end, 270 to 1080 (900 on a Logitech G29).
  The game's wheel turns all the way at 135 each way, as the arcade's does, and stays at its end past them;
  at 270, the default, steering is as before.
- **INPUT TEST**: the wheel, the gas and the brake as the game sees them, from -100% to 100% and from 0% to
  100%, to see where the wheel reaches its ends and the pedals their floor.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
