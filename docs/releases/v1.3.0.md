OutRunners 1.3.0: a racing wheel with its pedals and shifter apart, FULL and REVERSE in their right colours, and
a steadier NETWORK. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `orunners.zip` (MAME's
ROM set, World) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts. The devices' games
(Switch, Android, Anbernic) have to be made again with the tools in their packages: the ones made before are
not taken.

**New**

- **OPTIONS > STEERING > SET UP WHEEL with pedals and a shifter of their own** (#28): a wheel, pedals and a
  shifter plugged in on USB each are set up together. Turn the wheel, press the pedals and the buttons it asks
  for on whichever device they are: the wheel stays player 1's and gets the force feedback. A wheel with
  everything on it is set up as before.
- **OPTIONS > 2P MUSIC** (#22): in 2 PLAYERS, the left seat's radio alone, or both songs at once as the two
  cabinets play them.

**Fixed**

- **& KNUCKLES > FULL and REVERSE** (#25): the sky going black over a hill in REVERSE, the lanes eaten by orange
  zigzags, a red road, black blocks in the sky while the scenery changes, and the kangaroos at REVERSE's finish
  showing grey. Each stage's background now shows whole, with the colours it has in ARCADE.
- **WIDE through a change of scenery**: the old scenery no longer stays in the middle of the screen until the new
  one replaces it at once (the canyon on the road to Niagara). **Niagara's falls** no longer blink: their spray
  is drawn see-through every frame.
- **NETWORK** (#34): there is no pause in a network race any more (it stopped your car while the others raced
  on). A race whose host leaves just after its START now goes on for the others, who waited for good before.
  On Android the game holds Wi-Fi's multicast lock while NETWORK is open, so phones that missed the rooms on
  the network should find them now.
- `extract_assets.py` writes and deletes nothing outside its `assets` folder (#24).

Not checked on the devices themselves: SET UP WHEEL with a real wheel, pedals and shifter apart (checked with
SDL's virtual joysticks), and NETWORK on Android phones (checked with two games on one computer).

Known, still open: in REVERSE's finish the kangaroos at the sides of the road are hidden behind the crowd
(#29); some changes of scenery in FULL and REVERSE still cut at once (#30).

`README.txt` in each package says what its tools need. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
