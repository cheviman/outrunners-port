OutRunners 1.3.2: QUIT GAME stops the race's sound, WIDE follows your screen, and RecompLauncher can set the game
up for you. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `orunners.zip` (MAME's ROM set,
World) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts. The packages' names now carry
the version (`outrunners-v1.3.2-windows-x64.zip`).

**Fixed**

- **QUIT GAME** (#39): the engine and the race's music no longer go on at the title after QUIT GAME in the pause,
  nor after RESTART.

**New**

- **OPTIONS > VIDEO > WIDE AUTO** (#40), the new default on computers: the picture's width follows the shape of
  the screen the game is on, and changes with it: 16:10 on a Steam Deck in your hands, 16:9 docked to a TV.
  Settings that already say another WIDE keep it.
- **Jingle Bell**, the radio's hidden song, is on the way round of << and >>, between Last Wave and Magical Sound
  Shower. The arcade plays it for both pressed together, which a controller's pad cannot do.
- **A PlayStation controller's own buttons** on OPTIONS > CONTROLS: cross, circle, square, triangle, L1, R1, L2
  and R2 for a DualShock or a DualSense.
- **RecompLauncher** ([recomplauncher.com](https://recomplauncher.com/game/outrunners-port)): the packages for
  computers carry `recomplauncher.json`, so the launcher asks for `orunners.zip` once, sets the game up and
  shows its video settings on the game's page. Started with `--game` and the ROM set's path, the game takes its
  assets from there, for a shortcut too.
- **A log**: `outrunners.log` (`orunners.log` for OutRunners Arcade), with the two runs before it, beside the game
  on Windows and in the game's folder elsewhere, to attach to a report when something goes wrong. A file
  `portable.txt` beside the game keeps everything there on Linux and macOS too.

Your saves stay as they are, and the devices' games made with 1.3.1's tools are still taken.

Not checked on the devices themselves: WIDE AUTO on a Steam Deck (checked on Windows), a real DualSense (checked
with RecompLauncher's setting for it), the log and `portable.txt` on Linux and macOS, and the game inside
RecompLauncher itself (checked as the launcher starts it).

Known, still open: in REVERSE's finish the kangaroos at the sides of the road are hidden behind the crowd (#29);
some changes of scenery in FULL and REVERSE still cut at once (#30).

`README.txt` in each package says what its tools need. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
