OutRunners 1.2.2: the route map beside the race whole again, CLASSIC without waiting for challengers, and a
package for each device. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `orunners.zip`
(MAME's ROM set, World) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts.

**Packages**

- **A package for each device.** The Switch, Android and Anbernic's handhelds now have their own:
  `outrunners-switch.zip`, `outrunners-android.zip` and `outrunners-anbernic.zip`. Each one has the device's game
  and the tool that makes yours of it with your ROM set, for Windows, macOS and Linux, with a README that says
  what to do. The Windows, Linux and macOS packages have the two games alone.

**Fixed**

- **The route map beside the race** (a tall screen, a foldable phone opened): since 1.2.0 it showed the sea and
  the landmarks with no land or roads under them. Checked on a Galaxy Z Fold 7.
- **& KNUCKLES > CLASSIC** with one player no longer waits 10 seconds for challengers after the car select:
  the race starts once your choices are made. A second player can still join with START while the first one
  chooses.

Not checked on the devices themselves: CLASSIC (checked on the computer), and the macOS and Linux tools in
the new packages (the same tools as before, now packed beside each device's game).
