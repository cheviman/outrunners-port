OutRunners 1.3.1: updating no longer stops the game, and SET UP WHEEL takes a handbrake. As before, nothing of
the game comes in these packages: keep your own `orunners.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World) beside the game, and it
takes its assets out of it the first time it starts.

**Fixed**

- **Updating** (#37): the game no longer stops at "the assets pack is of another version of the game" after an
  update. The pack of its assets the game writes beside its files (`outrunners.assets`, which the devices'
  tools take) is written again when it is another version's, and the assets are taken out of the ROM set
  again where they are another version's too. On macOS it is in Application Support, so every update met it.

**New**

- **OPTIONS > STEERING > SET UP WHEEL takes an axis** for SHIFT UP, SHIFT DOWN and START (#28): a handbrake or
  a lever, pulled and let go when it asks, counts as held past the middle of its travel.

**Underneath**

- The decompiled game reads and writes its memory as the arcade's V70 does on a processor of either byte
  order: the bookkeeping and its course records by halfwords, and two lists of the program's data at odd
  addresses as they are. The arcade's 16 scenarios are identical, picture and sound, on the PC and on PowerPC.
  Your saves stay as they are, and the devices' games made with 1.3.0's tools are still taken.

Not checked on the devices themselves: the update on a Mac (checked on Windows) and SET UP WHEEL with a real
handbrake (checked with SDL's virtual joysticks).

`README.txt` in each package says what its tools need. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
