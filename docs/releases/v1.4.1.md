OutRunners 1.4.1: FULL's and REVERSE's scenery changes without a cut, the radio's MUSIC button as the cabinet's
again, and three fixes from your reports on 1.4.0. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep
your own `orunners.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time
it starts. The packages, each platform and device in its own: `outrunners-v1.4.1-windows-x64.zip`,
`outrunners-v1.4.1-linux-x64.tar.gz`, `outrunners-v1.4.1-macos-universal.zip`,
`outrunners-v1.4.1-raspberrypi-arm64.tar.gz`, `outrunners-v1.4.1-switch.zip`, `outrunners-v1.4.1-android.zip`,
`outrunners-v1.4.1-ios.zip`, `outrunners-v1.4.1-anbernic.zip`, `outrunners-v1.4.1-r36s.zip`.

**Fixed**

- **Scenery changes in FULL and REVERSE** (#30): at a fork's change of stage the background no longer cuts at
  once (the canyon and its sky, San Francisco's skyline): the new scenery is whole from the start, and the old one
  fades out under it in less than half a second.
- **The radio's MUSIC button** (#61): the cabinet's again, from the car's song to the DJ's station and back to the
  same song. 1.4.0's way round everything (each song, then the station, then the first song again) is still there:
  OPTIONS > SOUND > MUSIC BUTTON > ALL SONGS, handy on the touch controls, which have no << and >>.
- **The mouse pointer** (#60): it no longer sits over the game (in the middle of a Raspberry Pi's screen with no
  mouse to move it): it hides once it has stood still for two seconds, and shows again as the mouse moves.
- **The version in OPTIONS** (#62, #21): whole with WIDE 16:10 and 16:9; its right end was cut off.

Your saves and settings stay as they are; MUSIC BUTTON starts at ARCADE. The assets are 1.4.0's: on computers the
game keeps them, and the devices' games made with 1.4.0's tools are still taken.

Not checked on the devices themselves: the pointer under Batocera on a Raspberry Pi (the same code as in the TMNT
port), and FULL's and REVERSE's fade on the devices (checked frame by frame on Windows, at 4:3 and in WIDE).

`README.txt` in each package says what its tools need. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
