OutRunners 1.1: fixes for widescreen and FULL, a friendlier CLASSIC, and new options. As before, nothing of
the game comes in these packages: keep your own `orunners.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World) beside the game, and it
takes its assets out of it the first time it starts. The devices' games (Switch, Android, Anbernic) are made
again with the tools in the package.

**Fixed**

- **Widescreen backgrounds at the stage changes**: the clouds and the scenery behind the road no longer show
  cut or half-swapped at the sides of WIDE 16:9 and 20:9 after the pre-stage and at each checkpoint (the game
  swaps the background a column at a time out of its own 320 pixels, which WIDE's sides show).
- **FULL's and REVERSE's stage changes**: the new background no longer replaces the old one in a frame (the
  clouds turning into the city at once, also in 4:3); it slides in as the arcade's does.

**Changed**

- **CLASSIC**: 5 seconds more than ARCADE in every part, at the start and at each checkpoint.
- **The route map beside the race** (an open Fold, a tall screen) in CLASSIC too.

**New in OPTIONS**

- **STEERING**: the sticks' DEAD ZONE and SENSITIVITY, and the SPEED of the wheel on keys and d-pads.
- **FPS**: the frames a second at the top right of the picture, and in the window's title on a computer.
- **The version** at the bottom right.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
