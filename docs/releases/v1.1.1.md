OutRunners 1.1.1: a game controller's triggers in CONTROLS, and RESTART in the pause. As before, nothing of
the game comes in these packages: keep your own `orunners.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World) beside the game, and it
takes its assets out of it the first time it starts. The devices' games (Switch, Android, Anbernic) are made
again with the tools in the package.

**Fixed**

- **CONTROLS with a game controller** (#2): the triggers can be bound to any control (press one halfway);
  by default the right one is the gas and the left one the brake, pressed as far as they go. A button bound
  to a control leaves that player's other controls. L3, R3, Guide and Share show their pictures (they showed
  an empty place, and the pedals a trigger whatever they had). A `controls.ini` from 1.1.0 keeps the triggers
  on the pedals.
- **Building from the source with a newer GCC** (#6): the reference tools no longer stop on `roundf` and the
  rest of the V60's float conversions.

**New**

- **RESTART in the pause** (#4): the same game again, the same mode, with the car and the transmission chosen
  before picked by themselves on the car select (REVERSE from the same goal). Not in NETWORK races.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
