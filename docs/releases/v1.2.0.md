OutRunners 1.2.0: a racing wheel's centring and vibration, NETWORK on a library the ports share, and the
decompilation's latest. As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `orunners.zip`
(MAME's ROM set, World) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts.

**Make your devices' games again.** The game's data is laid out anew in this version: a computer takes its
assets out of `orunners.zip` again by itself, but the Switch's, Android's and Anbernic's games made with an
earlier version's tools do not start with this one's. Make them again with the tools in this package.

**New in OPTIONS > STEERING**

- **CENTRING**: a racing wheel with force feedback comes back to the centre on a spring, as the cabinet's did,
  from OFF to 100% of the wheel's strongest (50% at first). Without it a wheel like a Thrustmaster T300RS turns
  loose: the arcade game never pushed the wheel itself.
- **VIBRATION**: the arcade's one force, the cabinet's wheel motor, which shakes the wheel in a crash and off
  the road: on a wheel with force feedback, or as a game controller's rumble (OFF to 100%, 50% at first; on the
  devices too).
- With both OFF the game leaves the wheel as its driver has it. OutRunners Arcade has both at 50%, and its test
  mode's STEERING WHEEL REACTION turns the shake off, as on the cabinet.

**NETWORK**

- The rooms and the race's datagrams are now [arcade-lan](https://github.com/cheviman/arcade-lan)'s, a library
  the ports of decompiled arcade games share: the same races, rooms and devices dropping out as before. A
  device of 1.2.0 does not see the rooms of an earlier version's: play with the same version on every device.

**And**

- The decompilation's latest: the groundwork for big-endian processors (PowerPC), toward ports to more
  systems. Nothing changes in the game.
- A shorter README.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
