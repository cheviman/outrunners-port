OutRunners 1.4.0: new platforms (Raspberry Pi, iPhone and iPad, R36S) and SDL 3 on every platform that has it.
As before, nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `orunners.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World)
beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts. The packages, each platform and
device in its own: `outrunners-v1.4.0-windows-x64.zip`, `outrunners-v1.4.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`,
`outrunners-v1.4.0-macos-universal.zip`, `outrunners-v1.4.0-raspberrypi-arm64.tar.gz`,
`outrunners-v1.4.0-switch.zip`, `outrunners-v1.4.0-android.zip`, `outrunners-v1.4.0-ios.zip`,
`outrunners-v1.4.0-anbernic.zip`, `outrunners-v1.4.0-r36s.zip`.

**Fixed**

- **Northern lights** (#32): the northern lights of stage 26 (and a picture of the route map) no longer blink: the
  arcade draws them every other frame; the console version draws them see-through instead, as it already did with
  Niagara's spray.
- **REVERSE from AUSTRALIA** (#58): the start line's stands and people no longer stand on the harbour's water.
- **A racing wheel's centring** (#46): its spring no longer goes a couple of bends into a race (a Logitech G920's):
  the game runs it again where the device says it stopped.

**New**

- **New platforms**: Raspberry Pi 3 and later (64-bit Raspberry Pi OS), iPhone and iPad (an unsigned .ipa to
  sideload, e.g. with AltStore or Sideloadly), and R36S handheld (ArkOS with PortMaster), each with its own
  package (#47, PR #41).
- **SDL 3** (#47): on every platform that has it (Windows, Linux, macOS, Switch, Android, Raspberry Pi, iPhone
  and iPad); the Anbernic and R36S keep their firmware's SDL 2 through a small layer.
- **WIDE 21:9** (#44): for ultrawide monitors (with 16:10, 16:9 and 20:9). WIDE now moves the game's
  own screen edges: the game no longer removes or pops in scenery in plain sight at the sides (Niagara's spray,
  the landmarks, the title's palm trees, the cars crossing the road).
- **OPTIONS > SOUND** (#57): VOLUME, plus MUSIC, EFFECTS and VOICE apart, 0 to 10 each; e.g. the radio's songs
  over the DJ, or the voices off.
- **The radio**: the MUSIC button (space bar by default) now goes round everything: each song, then the DJ's
  station, then the first song again. << and >> still step through the songs (and the hidden Jingle Bell).
- **OPTIONS > CONTROLS** (#56, #42): the key or button you press takes the control's place, and the sticks'
  directions can be pedals (e.g. the right stick as gas and brake on the Switch).
- **2 PLAYERS with WIDE**: each screen's place in the window as wide as WIDE's, not the board's 320 between
  black bars.
- **OPTIONS > STEERING > SPEED's INSTANT** (#45): the keys' and pads' wheel all the way at once, and back to the
  centre at once.

Your saves and settings stay as they are; the new SOUND settings start at 5, the game's own sound. On computers
the assets of 1.3.2 are of another version: the game takes them out of orunners.zip again by itself the first
time 1.4.0 starts. The devices' games made with 1.3.2's tools must be made again with 1.4.0's (an older one says
"the assets pack is of another version of the game").

Not checked on the devices themselves: a Raspberry Pi (checked in an emulator, QEMU, the same frame by frame as
Windows), an iPhone or iPad (built, and run on the iOS simulator in the CI), a Switch, a phone, the R36S and the
Anbernic (their builds checked, and the Anbernic's game run natively on Linux over SDL 2), a Mac, a racing wheel
(#46: checked against a stand-in) and an ultrawide monitor. Played by hand on Windows.

Known, still open: some changes of scenery in FULL and REVERSE still cut at once (#30).

`README.txt` in each package says what its tools need. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
