OutRunners 1.2.1: VSYNC, WIDE 16:10 for the Steam Deck, and the phone vibrating in a crash. As before, nothing
of the game comes in these packages: keep your own `orunners.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World) beside the game, and
it takes its assets out of it the first time it starts. The devices' games (Switch, Android, Anbernic) are made
again with the tools in the package.

**New**

- **OPTIONS > VIDEO > VSYNC** (#19): each frame waits for the screen's refresh, so the picture no longer tears.
  It is on at first, and OutRunners Arcade always has it. The game keeps the arcade's 60 frames a second on any
  screen: on a 120 or 144 Hz one each frame shows twice or so, and on one a little slower than 60 a frame is left
  out now and then, so that the sound never breaks up.
- **OPTIONS > VIDEO > WIDE 16:10**: the picture as wide as a Steam Deck's screen (1280 x 800), beside 16:9 and
  20:9. On Android, WIDE follows the screen's shape and now takes 16:10 too, for a screen between 3:2 and 16:9.
- **The phone vibrates** with the cabinet's wheel motor, in a crash and off the road, when you play on the touch
  controls, at OPTIONS > STEERING > VIBRATION's strength. **STEERING > PHONE VIBRATION** turns it off. With a
  game controller, the controller rumbles instead.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
