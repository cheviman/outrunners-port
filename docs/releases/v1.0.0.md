The first release of the OutRunners port: Sega's 1992 arcade game as a native game, built on its
decompilation and checked frame by frame against MAME. Nothing of the game comes in these packages: keep
your own `orunners.zip` (MAME's ROM set, World) beside the game, and it takes its assets out of it the first
time it starts.

**The packages** (Windows, Linux, macOS for Apple silicon and Intel) hold both games and the tools that make
the devices' games with your ROM set:

- **OutRunners**: the console version. ARCADE, 2 PLAYERS (side by side, one over the other, or facing each
  other on a tablet), NETWORK, & KNUCKLES (CLASSIC, TIME ATTACK with its ghost, FULL, REVERSE), OPTIONS
  (CONTROLS, DIFFICULTY, TIME, VIDEO with PIXELS and WIDE 16:9 or 20:9, VOLUME, SOUND TEST), the pause.
- **OutRunners Arcade**: the arcade game as it is, both cabinet screens side by side.
- **OutRunners NSP** (Nintendo Switch, with your console's keys), **OutRunners APK** (Android, with a key of
  yours) and **OutRunners Anbernic** (the H700 handhelds' PORTS).

**NETWORK**: up to four devices on the same local network race together, computers, phones and the Switch
alike: one hosts a room, the others join it, the host's START races them all. A device that stops being
heard leaves the race after 3 seconds, and the race goes on; if it hosted, the next one hosts on.

`README.txt` in the package says what each tool needs. What a tool makes is yours alone: never share it.
